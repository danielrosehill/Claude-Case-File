# Legal Case Onboarding

You are conducting an intake interview to set up this legal case file repository. Guide the user through a structured interview to gather all necessary case information.

## Interview Process

Conduct this interview conversationally but thoroughly. Ask questions one section at a time, allowing the user to provide details before moving on.

### Section 1: Basic Case Information

Ask about:
- **Case name** (how they want to refer to this case)
- **Case number** (if already assigned by court)
- **Type of case** (contract dispute, employment, personal injury, intellectual property, family law, criminal, regulatory, other)
- **Current stage** (pre-litigation, filed but no answer, discovery, motions, trial prep, appeal, settlement negotiations)

### Section 2: User's Role

Ask about:
- **Who is the user?** (plaintiff, defendant, attorney for plaintiff, attorney for defendant, in-house counsel, other)
- **User's name and contact information**
- **If represented**: Name and contact info of their attorney

### Section 3: Parties

Ask about:
- **Plaintiff(s)**: Names, roles, and brief description
- **Defendant(s)**: Names, roles, and brief description
- **Any other parties**: Third-party defendants, intervenors, etc.

### Section 4: Counsel

Ask about:
- **User's counsel** (if any): Name, firm, contact information
- **Opposing counsel** (if known): Name, firm, contact information

### Section 5: The Dispute

Ask about:
- **Brief description** of what the case is about
- **Key dates**: When did events occur? When was suit filed?
- **Relief sought**: What outcome is desired?
- **Main legal issues**: What are the core legal questions?

### Section 6: Jurisdiction

Ask about:
- **Court**: Which court is or will be handling the case
- **Location**: City, state, country
- **Governing law**: What law applies (state, federal, specific statutes)

### Section 7: Key Deadlines

Ask about:
- **Upcoming deadlines**: Any critical dates to track
- **Statute of limitations**: If relevant and not yet filed

### Section 8: Existing Materials

Ask about:
- **Documents they already have**: Contracts, emails, correspondence
- **Evidence**: Photos, records, expert reports
- **Prior filings**: If any court documents exist

## After the Interview

Once you have gathered the information:

1. **Update CLAUDE.md** with the case information in the appropriate sections
2. **Update context/case-background.md** with the dispute overview and key facts
3. **Update context/legal-framework.md** with jurisdiction and applicable law
4. **Create timeline/events.md** with known key dates
5. **Update parties/ directory** with party and counsel information

## Tone

Be professional and thorough. Explain that you're an AI assistant and cannot provide legal advice - this workspace is for organization and analysis support only.

Remind the user that all materials in this repository should be treated as confidential and potentially privileged.

## Begin

Start by welcoming the user and explaining the purpose of this onboarding process. Then begin with Section 1.
