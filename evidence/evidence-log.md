## Entry Template

When adding new evidence, add a row with:

- **ID**: Exhibit or DF number (e.g., EX-001, DF-001)
- **Filename**: Full filename as stored
- **Date Added**: YYYY-MM-DD format
- **Source**: Where the evidence came from
- **SHA-256 Verified**: ✓ or ✗
- **Added By**: Who added the evidence
- **Notes**: Any relevant notes about the evidence
